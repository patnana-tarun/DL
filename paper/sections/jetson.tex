\textbf{Scope: no on-device measurements.} No number in this paper was measured on a Jetson; the runtime is
\emph{desktop-validated and Jetson-oriented}, and the timings above characterise the software path on a desktop GPU
or CPU, not TensorRT on an embedded module. Latency, power, thermal behaviour and the contention between detector and
VLM on the shared 8\,GB memory of an Orin are therefore not established here and are the subject of future work
(Sec.~\ref{sec:conclusion}).

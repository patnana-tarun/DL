Table~\ref{tab:det} summarises (precision--recall curves in Fig.~\ref{fig:pr}, Appendix~\ref{app:figs}) detection on the adaptation scene. Fine-tuning improves both
students: \yolo{} by \DeltaYoloFtPt{} \AP{} and \rfdetr{} by \DeltaRfFtPt{}, whose COCO-pretrained Nano model misses
most of the small, distant pedestrians (\APsm{}~\ApSmallRfPt). Fine-tuned \yolo{} leads fine-tuned \rfdetr{} by
\DeltaFtFt{} \AP{} (block CI \ci{\TcAaCbbBFtFtLo}{\TcAaCbbBFtFtHi}, \TcAaCbbBFtFtP); the gap is concentrated at strict
IoU (\APs{} \ApSeventyFiveYoloFt{} vs.\ \ApSeventyFiveRfFt) and on small people (\APsm{} \ApSmallYoloFt{} vs.\
\ApSmallRfFt), while \APf{} is close (\ApFiftyYoloFt{} vs.\ \ApFiftyRfFt). These are the two models'
\emph{deployment configurations} (1280 vs.\ 640\,px input); Sec.~\ref{sec:res} separates input size from
architecture. Fine-tuned \yolo{} scores \ApTrainYoloFt{} / \ApValidYoloFt{} / \ApTestYoloFt{} on train / valid /
test, and its train--test gap exceeds its pretrained counterpart's by \ExcessYoloFt{}; \rfdetr{} shows no excess
gap (\ExcessRfFt). The independent cross-check passes \XcPass/\XcTotal{} detection checks\ifHaveTrackXc{} and
\XtPass/\XtTotal{} tracking checks\fi.



\textbf{Label bias.} Each student scores best against its own family's teacher (last columns of
Table~\ref{tab:det}): under RF-DETR-Large-only labels the fine-tuned students differ by only \TbGapRfL{} \AP{}, under
YOLO26x-only labels by \TbGapYx. The fused labels favour \yolo{}, so the ranking holds ``under these pseudo-labels''.

\textbf{How much do correlated frames matter?} Table~\ref{tab:tci} repeats the analysis under every resampling
scheme. Treating the \TcAaFrames{} aa1 test frames as independent understates the standard error of \AP{} by a
factor of \TcAaRatioMin--\TcAaRatioMax{} (5\,s blocks), i.e.\ the split carries the information of roughly
\TcAaNeff{} independent frames. The Politis--White block length is \TcAaAutoBlock{} frames
(\TcAaAutoBlockS\,s) on aa1 and \TcHoAutoBlock{} frames (\TcHoAutoBlockS\,s) on the held-out video.
On the held-out video the automatic length (\TcHoAutoBlockS\,s) exceeds the 10\,s the review suggested, and the
standard error grows up to \TcHoCbbAutoYoloFtRatio$\times$. The intervals widen accordingly, but no conclusion
changes: every paired difference reported in this paper keeps its sign and excludes zero under every scheme,
including the longest blocks (Table~\ref{tab:tci}).

\textbf{Latency.} Uploading the uint8 frame once, GPU pre-processing, a fused FP16 network and GPU NMS make \yolo{}
\SpeedupYolo$\times$ faster than the stock path at unchanged AP (\LatYoloDep\,ms per 720p frame); FP16, TorchScript and
GPU pre-processing make \rfdetr{} \SpeedupRf$\times$ faster (\LatRfDep\,ms). The NMS-free head of \yolo{} is faster but
loses \NmsFreeApLoss{} \AP{} in this crowded scene, so the deployed graph keeps the one-to-many head with NMS (Table~\ref{tab:lat},
Appendix~\ref{app:latency}).

\textbf{Human ground truth.} \ifHaveHuman\input{sections/human_results.tex}\else
Every number above is agreement with the teacher ensemble. The earlier label audit (Sec.~\ref{sec:heldout}) was made
by an AI assistant from crops and is therefore not independent. We release a protocol and tooling for an
independent human evaluation set that is being annotated: \HuFramesPlanned{} detection frames drawn with a fixed
seed from the \emph{test} portions of all four scenes (stratified by crowd density and illumination) and two 5\,fps
tracking clips (the aa1 tracking window and 30\,s of the held-out video). Two annotators label every frame
\emph{blind and from scratch} in a browser tool that never shows machine boxes (pre-annotation anchors annotators to
the machine~\cite{berzak2016anchoring}), following MOT16 identity rules~\cite{milan2016mot16} and Caltech ignore
semantics~\cite{dollar2012pedestrian} (people under 20\,px, unseparable groups and reflections are ignore regions on
which detections are neither rewarded nor penalised); a third person adjudicates only their disagreements. The
analysis is fixed in advance: inter-annotator matched F1 and IoU (the human ceiling), teacher precision and recall
by person height, every model's AP against human labels, the Kendall~$\tau$ between the model rankings under human
and pseudo labels, annotator-vs-annotator HOTA/IDF1 on the clips, and line-crossing precision/recall at
$\pm$0.5/1/2\,s. Until these labels exist, absolute numbers in this paper are agreement with the teachers, and
only comparisons under the same reference are claimed.\fi
